THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

Hi, I'm Kaedn Rhys Mailand.

I'm a creative storyteller who works across filmmaking, editing, and performance. I enjoy shaping stories from both sides of the camera, whether I'm guiding a project through the edit or stepping into a role myself.

My work ranges from thoughtful, message-driven videos to playful promotional projects. What connects them is a love for stories with personality and purpose.

This is a growing home for my projects, experiments, and the work I'm excited to share next.
